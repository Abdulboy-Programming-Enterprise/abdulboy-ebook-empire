# Custom Books - User Guide

## What Are Custom Books?

Custom books are professionally written books tailored to your specific requirements. Perfect for:

- Business books
- Personal memoirs
- Educational materials
- Brand storytelling
- Ghostwriting projects

## How It Works

### Step 1: Submit Request

1. Go to **Custom Book Booking**
2. Fill out the request form:
   - Book title (working title)
   - Genre
   - Approximate word count
   - Deadline
   - Budget range
   - Detailed requirements
3. Upload any reference materials (optional)
4. Submit request

### Step 2: Review & Quote

- Our team reviews your request (24-48 hours)
- We provide a quote and timeline
- If acceptable, you approve the proposal

### Step 3: Payment

- 50% deposit required to start
- Remainder upon completion
- Secure payment via Stripe or OPay

### Step 4: Writing Process

1. Research phase (1-2 weeks)
2. Outline creation
3. Chapter-by-chapter delivery
4. Revisions based on feedback
5. Final delivery

### Step 5: Delivery

- Receive completed book in PDF, EPUB, and DOCX formats
- Copyright transferred to you
- Publish under your name

## What to Include in Your Request

### Essential Information

- **Purpose**: Why are you creating this book?
- **Target Audience**: Who will read it?
- **Tone**: Professional, casual, inspirational?
- **Key Messages**: What points must be covered?

### Helpful Information

- **Competitor books** in similar style
- **Sample chapters** of preferred writing style
- **Outline** if you have one
- **Research materials** or references
