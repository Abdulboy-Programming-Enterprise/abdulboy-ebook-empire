# Buying Books - User Guide

## How to Purchase a Book

### One-Time Purchase

1. **Find a book** you want to buy
2. Click **"Buy Now"** on the book page
3. Review order summary
4. Select payment method (Credit Card or OPay)
5. Enter payment details
6. Confirm purchase

### Payment Methods

| Method | Countries | Processing Time |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| Credit Card (Stripe) | Global | Instant |
| OPay | Nigeria | Instant |
| PayPal | Global | Instant |

### After Purchase

- Book appears in **"My Library"** immediately
- Receipt sent to your email
- Can be read online or downloaded (Premium only)

### Cancel Subscription

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Subscription**
2. Click **"Cancel Subscription"**
3. Confirm cancellation
4. Access continues until end of billing period

## Refund Policy

### Eligible for refund within 30 days if:

- Book is defective (missing pages, formatting issues)
- Duplicate purchase
- Accidental purchase (within 24 hours)

### Not eligible for refund if:

- Book has been downloaded
- More than 30 days since purchase
- Free books

### Request Refund

1. Contact support@abdulboy-ebook.com
2. Provide order number
3. Explain reason
4. Refund processed within 5-7 business days

## Gift Cards

### Purchase Gift Card

1. Go to **Gift Cards** page
2. Select amount ($10, $25, $50, $100)
3. Enter recipient email (optional)
4. Complete payment
5. Gift code sent via email

### Redeem Gift Card

1. Go to **Redeem Gift Card** page
2. Enter gift code
3. Credit added to your account
4. Use for future purchases

## View Order History

## FAQ

### Why was my payment declined?

- Insufficient funds
- Incorrect card details
- Bank security block
- Try different payment method

### When will my subscription renew?

- Monthly plans renew on same day each month
- You'll receive email reminder 7 days before
- Cancel anytime before renewal date

### How do I change payment method?

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Billing**
2. Click **"Update Payment Method"**
3. Enter new payment details
