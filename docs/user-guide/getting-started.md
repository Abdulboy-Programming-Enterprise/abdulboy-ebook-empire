# Getting Started - User Guide

## Welcome to Abdulboy Ebook Empire

Thank you for joining our platform! This guide will help you get started.

## Creating an Account

### Step 1: Sign Up

1. Visit [https://abdulboy-ebook.com/signup.html](https://abdulboy-ebook.com/signup.html)
2. Enter your email address
3. Create a strong password (min 8 characters, mix of letters and numbers)
4. Enter your full name
5. Click "Sign Up"

### Step 2: Verify Email

1. Check your email inbox
2. Click the verification link
3. Your account is now active!

## Finding Books

### Browse All Books

- Click "Books" in the navigation menu
- Browse through our collection
- Filter by category, price, or language

### Search for Books

1. Type keywords in the search bar (title, author, or genre)
2. Press Enter or click the search icon
3. Use filters to narrow results

### AI Recommendations

- Your dashboard shows personalized recommendations
- Based on your reading history and purchases
- Updates daily

## Reading Books

### Free Books

- Click on any free book
- Click "Read Now"
- Read directly in your browser

### Purchased Books

1. Click "Buy Now" on any book
2. Complete checkout
3. Book appears in "My Library"
4. Click "Read" to start reading

### Subscription Access

- Premium members get unlimited access
- Read any book in our catalog
- Download for offline reading (Premium feature)

## Book Preview

- Click "Preview" on any book
- Read first 10-50 pages for free
- Decide if you want to purchase

## Bookmark & Progress

- Your reading progress saves automatically
- Resume from where you left off
- Bookmarks sync across devices

## Subscription Plans

### Free Plan
- Access to free books only
- 3 books per month limit
- Limited previews

### Basic Plan ($9.99/month)
- Access to all books
- 10 books per month
- Download up to 5 books

### Premium Plan ($19.99/month)
- Unlimited access
- Unlimited downloads
- Offline reading
- Priority support
- Early access to new releases

## Custom Book Service

Need a book written just for you?

1. Go to "Custom Book" page
2. Fill out the request form
3. Specify genre, length, deadline, budget
4. Submit request
5. Our team will contact you within 48 hours
